[ 03 / About me ]

A psychologist stitching textiles, a designer listening to people.

I'm an aspiring Textile & Fashion Designer at NIFT Gandhinagar, blending technical craft with a parallel BA in Psychology from IGNOU.

I specialise in human-centric design that balances aesthetic sophistication with an understanding of consumer behaviour — bringing together fabric structures, textile chemistry, color psychology, and trend forecasting. My work investigates sustainability, sensory storytelling, and tactile equity in fashion. I love when a garment can be felt before it's seen.
