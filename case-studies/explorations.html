Hand & Loom.

A working journal of material experiments — handwoven samples, leather trials, and small craft objects made to keep my hands learning.

Discipline Weave · Leather · Embroidery
Duration Ongoing
Role Maker · researcher
Outputs 30+ swatches · 5 objects
[ Intro ]

This is the work that doesn't fit into a single project — the practice behind the practice. Loom samples between briefs, leather offcuts turned into small objects, embroidery exercises that turned into vocabularies for later collections.

Material understanding cannot be rushed. This page is here to show the slow part.

01 / 07

A year of small handwoven samples — testing structure, density, and tonal contrast on a four-shaft loom.

[ 01 — Weave ]

Slow structure.

Each sample sits with a small index card — yarn count, sett, draft, and a short note on what it taught me. The collection is now a private library I draw from when starting any new project.

[ 02 — Leather ]

Hide as canvas.

A short leatherwork module turned into a small obsession with stitch density, edge finishes, and the way pigment soaks into vegetable-tanned hide.

02 / 07

Five small leather objects made over the term — each finished by hand, edges burnished with beeswax.

[ 03 — Embroidery ]

Stitch studies.

A grid of stitch experiments — chain, satin, French knot, kantha — each repeated until the hand learns its rhythm. These exercises are now part of how I prototype surface for any garment project.
