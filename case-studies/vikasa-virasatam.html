Case Study 06 July 2025 Fashion showcase Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi

Vikāsa-Virasatam.

Participating in a showcase by NIFT Gandhinagar, highlighting the dialogue between heritage and contemporary design in Indian textiles.

Event Global Outreach Summit 2025
Role Model
Focus Heritage textiles · modern drape
Venue Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi
[ Intro ]

In July 2025, I had the opportunity to participate in a showcase by NIFT Gandhinagar by modeling a garment from our Resource Centre collection at the “Vikāsa-Virasatam” fashion show for BSL excellence awards during the Global Outreach Summit 2025, held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi.

The showcased ensemble featured a traditional saree reimagined with a modern drape, embodying the event’s central theme: the dialogue between heritage and contemporary design in Indian textiles.

[ 01 — The Showcase ]

Heritage on the runway.

The runway presentation was a celebration of Indian craftsmanship and evolving design. Walking in the show required a deep appreciation for the garment's construction, ensuring the modern drape was presented with the confidence and grace suited for such a prominent platform.

01 / 02

Runway moments from the showcase, highlighting the modern drape of the traditional saree.

[ 02 — Behind the Scenes ]

Preparation & presence.

Participating in this show offered valuable insight into styling, presentation, and stage presence. It was an exercise in understanding how a garment moves and how to best showcase its unique qualities to an audience.

02 / 02

Behind the scenes with the team, preparing for the runway presentation.

[ Reflection ]

Innovation & legacy.

The experience highlighted how innovation can coexist with cultural legacy. It was an inspiring opportunity to be part of an event that celebrated the rich craftsmanship and evolving spirit of Indian fashion, while also learning the nuances of runway presentation.
