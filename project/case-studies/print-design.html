Case Study 03 2024 Surface Print Repeat development

In Bloom.

A surface pattern series exploring botanical abstraction — repeat development, colour story, and the quiet music of a well-tiled motif.

Discipline Surface design · Print
Duration 8 weeks
Role Designer
Outputs 6 prints · 3 colourways each
[ Intro ]

In Bloom takes a single set of botanical drawings and asks: how many surfaces can one motif live on?

Each print in the series begins from the same hand-drawn study — a fig leaf, a poppy seed, a pomegranate cross-section — and is then re-tiled across half-drop, brick, mirror and damask repeats, in three distinct colourways.

01 / 08

The starting point — original botanical studies in ink and watercolour, scanned and vectorised in Illustrator.

[ 01 — Repeats ]

Tile, mirror, drop.

Six print families, each derived from the same set of motifs but tiled differently — proving that repeat structure is its own design choice, as expressive as colour or scale.

02 / 08

Print 05 photographed at scale — the same drawing now reads as a quiet, all-over surface.

[ 02 — Colourways ]

Three moods.

Each print is delivered in three colourways — a warm earth, a cool botanical, and a high-contrast graphic — so a buyer can pick the temperature that fits their season.
